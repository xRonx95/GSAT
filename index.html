Recommended Essay Structure

Introduction

Introduce the subject, provide background information, and present your thesis.

Body Paragraph 1

Explain your first main argument with relevant supporting evidence.

Body Paragraph 2

Discuss your second argument and provide examples.

Conclusion

Summarize the main arguments, reinforce the thesis, and provide a final insight.
